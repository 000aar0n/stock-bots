How the chain runs

Patch bots watch their names and log a call with its cause written down. Rival bots on the same name compete, and a disagreement flags the name contested. Section captains pre rank their sector and escalate only the best calls. JUDGE approves or blocks, BOUNCER can veto on risk, ODDS ranks an uncalibrated heuristic, TREASURER and LEDGER settle the budget inside the $200k envelope. Sessions place the orders and verify every fill. HANDS stays in dry run.

JUDGE rule: there is only one JUDGE. Every approval and block is scored, including what blocked calls would have made. Only completed paper outcomes across multiple sessions count. JUDGE reviews are advisory; the unattended runner never removes its risk gate. Status today: 0 scored decisions, 20 needed. Nobody is proven yet.

Night rule (hybrid): the day book goes flat by the close. Only a small sleeve of proven winners may ride overnight, capped at about a third of the book. Stat arb pairs are exempt.
