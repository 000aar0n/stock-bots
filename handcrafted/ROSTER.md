# Handcrafted Roster: 100 bots, built like a peasant

One hundred individually written bots, each its own file in
`handcrafted/`, each with a distinct name and a genuinely distinct
rule set, built from the shared `ta.py` (WICK) and `scanner.py`
(FALCON) primitives. Design grid: strategy families (momentum,
mean reversion, breakout, fade and short, gap play, range trade,
relative strength, volume climax, volatility squeeze, earnings
drift, baskets, index relative, swing, intraday) crossed with
scopes (single stock craftsmen, pairs, theme baskets, the whole
universe) and timeframes (5 minute, daily, multi day swing).

Hold styles: the city is DAY TRADER FIRST (see the README). A bot
marked swing declared hold_style "swing" in the registry because
its rule set honestly needs days (pairs, slow mean reversion,
range traversals, rotations); intraday bots' exits are reachable
inside the session. SEXTANT declares "none": it never trades.

Every bot logs paper calls to `output/calls.jsonl` behind
`--log-calls` only, each call carrying its cause, and is registered
in `output/bots.json` as kind `handcrafted`, gen 1, born_equal true,
fitness null (unproven). DARWIN has NO exemption for them:
handcrafted bots breed and can be benched under the same thresholds
as factory bots (20 ranked calls total, 5 per bot), and under the
Equality Protocol the unproven cannot be benched or bred at all.
